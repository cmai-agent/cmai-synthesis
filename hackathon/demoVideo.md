# Demo Story

## 10-Second Pitch

`cmai` is a trusted AI operator for onchain community building. It helps a team manage support, collaborations, community and collection tasks, identity, and bounded wallet actions without giving an agent unlimited control.

Live public proof point: `https://x.com/cmai_agent`

## Primary Theme

- `trust`

## Secondary Themes

- `Agents that pay`
- `cooperate`

## Real User

The target user is a founder, creator, or small community team building an NFT or onchain community product.

Their pain is real:
- too many inbound messages
- too many coordination tasks
- too much manual community ops
- low trust in agents for money, access, and external-facing actions

## What Risk We Resolve

The agentic risk we are solving is:

How do you let an agent help with real onchain community building and NFT collection work without giving it unsafe autonomy over money, identity, access, and community trust?

## Product Answer

`cmai` gives the team a bounded operator:
- it can help handle community and collaboration workflows
- it can prepare and assist with onchain actions
- it uses approval boundaries for sensitive execution
- it reports actions clearly
- it ties important actions to Ethereum-native identity and receipts

## Why Ethereum, Not Plain Web2

Ethereum is necessary here because the product relies on:
- wallet identity
- ENS-based naming and presence
- NFT collection operations
- onchain transfers and swaps
- verifiable receipts for what the agent did

A normal Web2 assistant can coordinate text.
It cannot natively provide the same identity, ownership, payment, and auditability layer for onchain community building work.

## Partner Tooling Used For Real Value

- `Bankr` powers bounded wallet execution
- `Uniswap API` powers swap and bridge flows
- `ENS` powers identity operations
- `OpenSea` supports collection and marketplace operations

These are not decorative integrations. They are part of the working product path.

## End-to-End Demo Flow

Show one simple but complete story:

1. A community team uses `cmai` to manage a real NFT collection or onchain community workflow
2. `cmai` handles a community or collaborator request
3. `cmai` prepares the next operational action
4. A sensitive wallet-linked action is reviewed and approved
5. The action executes through the bounded wallet layer
6. The system reports the result with clear traceability
