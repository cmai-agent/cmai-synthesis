Community building is hard, manual, and hard to scale. It is still too time-consuming for humans to build and manage onchain communities, and AI still cannot be trusted with that work without explicit boundaries, approvals, and auditable actions.

`cmai` focuses on making that work more usable and trustworthy by helping with real NFT and onchain community operations while keeping sensitive actions bounded, reviewable, and tied to Ethereum-native identity and execution where relevant.
